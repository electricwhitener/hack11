The hackathon problem statement is:

$ARGUMENTS

Do this in order. Do not write any code yet.

**1. Read the statement literally.** List what is explicitly required versus what
is merely implied. Judges score against the stated ask first.

**2. Predict the crowd.** In one short paragraph: what will the median team build
for this? That is the baseline you must not resemble. This is a beginner
hackathon where most teams use coding agents, so the obvious build will appear
many times.

**3. Propose exactly 3 directions.** For each, give:
   - The one-sentence pitch.
   - The specific agentic mechanic that makes it different — see
     `docs/IDEA-PLAYBOOK.md` for the patterns worth using.
   - What the 90-second demo looks like, moment by moment.
   - Honest build risk in 36 hours: Low / Medium / High.
   - Why a judge remembers it.

**4. Recommend one.** Pick the one with the best ratio of memorability to risk,
and say plainly why the other two lose.

**5. Give the build plan** for the recommendation: the data model, the 3–6 agent
tools needed, the screens, and what to cut first if time runs short.

Be opinionated. A hedged answer is useless at hour zero.
